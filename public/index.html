    03 / REPRODUCIBLE BY DESIGN

    Follow every decision back to its source.

    01

    Freeze the evidence

    Compact snapshots preserve saved prediction scores and separately recorded assay labels. This website does not train a model or call a prediction service.

    02

    Rank, then budget

    Sort raw scores from highest to lowest, with phage identifiers breaking ties. Your budget selects the top scored pairs. Experimental labels never influence selection.

    03

    Propose, then test

    A deterministic heuristic favors distinct known sequence clusters within the shortlist. Sequence clusters are a diversity proxy, not evidence of complementary mechanisms.
