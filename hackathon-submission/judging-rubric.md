# Evidence to show the judges

The [criteria](https://eurekadev.devpost.com/rules) weight four dimensions equally at 25%. This guide points to evidence, not a guaranteed score or prize.

| Criterion | Project argument | Demo evidence | Repository evidence |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Innovation & Creativity | Saved predictions become a transparent budgeted plan, with failure inspection and shared-panel optimization. | Change budget, compare random baseline, explore a shared panel. | Planning/analysis modules, methods, export schema |
| Impact & Relevance | Researchers need to prioritize screens and judge whether rankings deserve trust. | Show the ECOR12 failure and a limited screening budget. | Source-backed problem, scientific boundaries |
| Execution & Technical Quality | Functional static site, deterministic algorithms, exact small searches, meaningful tests. | Switch banks/hosts, export a plan, inspect provenance. | Modular code, tests, checks, setup, Pages workflow |
| Presentation & Communication | One practical question connects problem, workflow, evidence, and limits. | Show failure and a second-bank example; end with an export and next study. | Short script, written description, build disclosure |

The pitch: **“Which phages should a lab test next, and what evidence supports that choice?”** Claim novelty in the workflow and evidence handling. Avoid first-of-its-kind, new therapy, new underlying model, or measured spending-reduction claims.

## Rehearse these questions

**Why show a failed model?** Confident-looking scores can fail on an external bank. Users should see that before planning experiments.

**Did you train the models here?** No. This site uses existing research predictions and assays. The new work is the website, integration, planning, analysis, tests, and deployment.

**Why combine ranks?** Raw scores are not calibrated across hosts. Reciprocal ranks give an explicit preference objective; it still does not predict biological coverage.

**Is the cocktail validated?** No. Individual assays are separate endpoints. The mixture needs its own experiments.

**What would establish impact?** A prospective comparison of a frozen selection policy with suitable baselines, including negative results and separately tested mixtures.

**How did you use AI?** Codex assisted implementation, inspection, testing, and writing. Explain the actual design decisions and human contributions accurately.
